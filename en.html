Featured Book

United Beyond Death

Blending love, grief, and the supernatural, United Beyond Death by Catte Coelho tells the story of Khalee and Kaze, two souls bound by fate. After Kaze disappears, Khalee tries to rebuild her life until, five years later, she finds him again... as a ghost with no memories.

Now, she must decide whether to help him recover his past or leave behind the love that defied death itself.
